CareerLens is a free AI career platform for developers and tech professionals. It provides resume ATS scoring, keyword gap analysis, job matching, interview question practice, salary insights, and career guidance.

Main pages:
- https://www.carrerlens.com/
- https://www.carrerlens.com/resume
- https://www.carrerlens.com/interview-questions
- https://www.carrerlens.com/salary
- https://www.carrerlens.com/blog

Use cases:
- Check whether a resume passes ATS screening
- Improve resume keywords and bullet points
- Prepare for technical and behavioral interviews
- Learn how to write a cover letter or cold email
- Compare salary expectations for tech roles
